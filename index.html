05 — Questions

Before you ask

How do I order from HADUM?

DM us on Instagram with a screenshot of the piece you want. We confirm size, price, payment and delivery with you personally in that chat, then prepare your order.

When is the new collection dropping?

Very soon. The first drop is announced first on Instagram — follow @hadumshop and turn on notifications so you see it before stock moves.

Where is HADUM based?

Khulna, Bangladesh. HADUM is designed here — heritage craft, made in Bangladesh, designed for modern life everywhere.

Do you show prices openly?

Yes — always. Every product post carries a clear price and full honest details. No “DM for price” games, ever.

What are payment and delivery options?

These are confirmed personally with you in the DM when you order, based on your location and the piece — so you always know the full cost before you commit.

Can I ask about a piece before ordering?

Of course — DM us anything. Materials, sizing, care, styling — we'd rather you ask than guess.
